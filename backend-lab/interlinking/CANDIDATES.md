## Contender-field note for `backend-query-ranking.md`

The filed latency race (bm25-bonus vs trigram prefilter) measures ms/query behind a correctness gate. Our `link-retrieval-recall` race (requests/) is its complement: recall@10-first, latency-gated, adding the RRF-hybrid and vector-arm contenders. If both run, the winner matrix is 2×2 (latency × recall) and the production choice should take the Pareto front, not either race alone.
