Benchmark Racetrack Filing & Execution Instructions

To launch candidate implementations against each other in the Racetrack evaluation runner:

  1. Drop a race request in racetrack/requests/<slug>.md matching the spec format: ```markdown # race: seat: question: metric: min gate:

## candidate: source: / approach:

## candidate: source: / approach: ```

  1. Execute ./run-race.sh requests/<slug>.md inside ~/coding/docker-envs/projects/evo-myworld/racetrack.
  2. Results will auto-publish to racetrack/results/<slug>.md and candidate scores will graduate to the winner slot.
