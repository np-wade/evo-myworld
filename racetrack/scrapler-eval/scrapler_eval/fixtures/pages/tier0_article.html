The Marathon Continues

by Ada Lovelace

Ownership is the throughline. You build your own block, your own tools, your own ecosystem, and you keep the equity. A static page like this needs no browser at all — the content is right here in the HTML, and a plain fetch that survives the TLS handshake will read every word.

The lesson for a scraper is simple: do not pay for a browser you do not need. When the bytes are already in the markup, the fastest correct tool wins.
